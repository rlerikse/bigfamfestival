# Big Fam 1.4.2 — Release Notes

## Improvements & Fixes
- **Pull-to-refresh on the schedule grid:** the horizontal (stage-by-stage) schedule view now reloads the latest set times when you pull down at the top — matching the list view.
- **Steadier schedule scrolling:** your vertical position in the grid is preserved when you switch between list and grid views, and the grid no longer loses its place on refresh, filter, or day changes.
