### [2026-08-17] ADR-019: Distance-threshold handoff to external map apps

### [2026-08-17] Horizontal schedule zoom controls (feature, no new ADR — UI-only)
`HorizontalScheduleView.tsx`'s fixed `PX_PER_MINUTE` density constant became `pxPerMinute` state (range 3.0–6.0 after dropping the two redundant zoomed-in steps; defaults to fully zoomed out). `rowHeight` scales down toward a compact `MIN_ROW_HEIGHT` in lockstep as you zoom out, converging on a denser Shambhala-reference-style grid.
